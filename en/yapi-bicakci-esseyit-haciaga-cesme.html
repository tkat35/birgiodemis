Historic Sites / Fountains

Bıçakçı Fountain

Bıçakçı Esseyit Hacı Ali Ağa Fountain

Show on map

It stands on Şehit Gürol Madan Street, opposite the Dervişağa Mosque. The Bıçakçı Fountain was built by Bıçakçı Esseyit Hacı Ali Ağa in 1807. Built of reused and rubble stone, the fountain's corner mouldings turn into a dentil (kirpi) cornice at the top. Its façade is set in a recess, within which a pointed-arched panel is also recessed; it has a marble fountain panel (ayna) with relief decoration and a spout hole. The building has an inscription. A Roman marble sarcophagus with garlands and bull heads, pierced at one corner, is used as the trough. Terracotta water pipes are found inside the fountain. Fountains like this were built in different parts of the town to supply drinking water to the people. Those who built the fountains also took on the upkeep of the water channels brought in by earthenware pipes.
