Historic Sites / Other Historic Buildings

Police Station Building

Show on map

The building in the centre of Birgi was opened in 1330 AH (1911/12 AD). Built in the neo-classical style, it was designed in the late Ottoman period in the Küçük Menderes basin as a two-storey, T-shaped building. On a triangular marble pediment above the entrance on the main façade is the building's inscription in Arabic script, in relief. It reads “BİRGİ JANDARMA KARAKOLU 1330” (Birgi Gendarmerie Station 1330). The building lies within the Birgi Urban Conservation Area and its conservation-oriented development plan.
