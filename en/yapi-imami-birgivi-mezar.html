Historic Sites / Tombs

Grave of İmam-ı Birgivi

Show on map

İmam-ı Birgivi Mehmet Efendi was born in Balıkesir in 1523. He received his first education from his father, studying Arabic, logic and some other sciences. He then went to Istanbul and studied with Küçük Şemseddin Efendi in the Mahmutpaşa neighbourhood. While a student of Abdurrahman Efendi, he received his licence (icazet) and the rank of professor (müderris). He was appointed professor at the madrasa built in Birgi by Atâullah Efendi of Birgi, the tutor of Sultan Selim II; he came to Birgi at the age of 41 and spent his life there teaching, guiding and writing, training many students. He is the author of 57 works. Never afraid to speak the truth, towards the end of his life Birgivi went to Istanbul again and advised the Grand Vizier Sokullu Mehmet Paşa to fight the injustices in the country. He died in a plague epidemic in Birgi in 1573 and was buried in Birgi.
