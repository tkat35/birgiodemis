About

A Walk in the Town: The Historical and Cultural Journey of Birgi

This site is the website of the outcome of the project “A Walk in the Town: The Historical and Cultural Journey of Birgi” (Şehrin İçinde Bir Gezinti: Birgi'nin Tarihi ve Kültürel Yolculuğu), carried out by students of the Ödemiş Science and Art Centre (BİLSEM) within the TÜBİTAK 2204 Research Projects programme with the support of the Ödemiş District Governorship.

The project aims to research and promote the historical and cultural heritage of Birgi, in the Ödemiş district of İzmir. Within the project a mobile app was developed first; its content was then moved to this website so that it can be reached from all phones and computers.
