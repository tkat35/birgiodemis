Historic Sites / Other Historic Buildings

Water Tower (Su Terazisi)

Show on map

In Birgi there are many works from the period of the principalities that represent the town's relationship with water. In Birgi, once the capital of the Aydınid Principality and later an Ottoman town, there are many works connected with water in almost every street (aqueducts, public fountains, water towers). One of these is the water tower (su terazisi) at the corner where İmam-ı Birgivi Street meets III. Beyzade Street. Still standing the test of time, the plain structure is 3.5 metres tall and built of rubble stone. It bears no inscription, and there is no information about when it was built. It probably dates from the period of the principalities.
