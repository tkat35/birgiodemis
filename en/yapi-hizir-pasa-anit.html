Hızır Paşa Monument

Show on map

The physician Celaleddin Hızır El Aydıni, who was chief physician at the Kalavun Hospital in Cairo, came to Birgi at the invitation of Aydınoğlu İsa Bey; he practised medicine here and also wrote more than ten books on medicine, logic, theology and Qur'anic commentary. His first Turkish medical book, written in a language the people could understand, introduced his own people to the science of medicine. The physician, known as Hacı Paşa, was so loved by the people that the place where he lived and was buried was named Hıdırlık. In the early years of the Republic, in 1934, a monument was erected in his memory on what is now Şehit Gürol Madan Street. He is commemorated every year on 14 March, Medicine Day, when his grave is visited.
