Privacy Policy

Scope

This policy applies to the birgiodemis.com.tr website and to the “Birgi” mobile app prepared for Android and iPhone. The website and the app have no commercial purpose.

Personal data collected

The app and the website do not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, log-ins, forms, advertising, analytics or tracking tools. The app does not use your contacts, photos or device ID.

Location

When you press the “My location” button on the map, your device’s location is obtained only with your permission and only while the map is open; it is shown on the map as a blue dot. When you open the “Nearby sites” list, your location is likewise used only while the list is open and only to sort the historic sites by their distance from you. Your location is used only on your device or in your browser; it is never sent to any server, recorded or shared. If you do not press these buttons, location is never requested; you can withdraw the permission at any time in your device or browser settings.

Map

The base map was prepared from OpenStreetMap data (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL) and is built into the app and the website; no external map service is contacted while the map is displayed.

Weather

The weather information on the home page is obtained from the Open-Meteo service (open-meteo.com) using the fixed coordinates of Birgi. No information about your location or identity is sent with this request; if there is no internet connection, the weather is not shown.

Promotional video

The Birgi promotional video (MEDYAPOZ, used with permission) is built into the mobile app and plays offline. On the website, the video is loaded from the site’s own server only when you tap the play button. No YouTube or other third-party service is used for the video, and no data is collected while you watch it.

External links

The “Get directions” and “Website” buttons open the link in the maps app or browser on your device. The privacy policies of those services apply.

Children

The app is suitable for users of all ages and does not collect any data from children either.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the current version will be published on this page. For questions, please use the developer contact address on the app’s Google Play and App Store page. Last updated: November 2026.
