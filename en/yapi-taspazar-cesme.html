Historic Sites / Fountains

Taşpazar Fountain

Show on map

It is in the Taşpazar neighbourhood of Birgi, Ödemiş. It was built by Hafsa Hatun, wife of the Ottoman Sultan Bayezid I (Yıldırım), who ensured its upkeep through the foundation she established. Built of reused and rubble stone, the fountain is a balanced piece of engineering: within a recess formed by three concentric round arches is a fountain panel with a wheel-of-fortune (çarkıfelek) motif in each of its two upper corners.
