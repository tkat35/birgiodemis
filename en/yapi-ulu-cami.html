Historic Sites / Mosques

Ulu Cami (Great Mosque)

Aydınoğlu Mehmet Bey Mosque

Show on map

It stands east of the Birgi stream, on Aydınoğlu Square at the end of the tree-lined road (Fatih Mehmet Bey Street) that runs through the town from south to north. To its south is the Ümmü Sultan Şah Tomb, to its east the İmam-ı Birgivi Madrasa and to its northeast the Ulu Cami Hamam. It shares the same courtyard with the Aydınoğlu Mehmet Bey Tomb right next to it. It was built by Aydınoğlu Mehmet Bey in 1312–13.
