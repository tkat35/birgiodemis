Birgi

A 700-year-old Turkish town at the foot of Bozdağ, once the capital of the Beylik of Aydın

With its stone-walled, red-tiled houses and wooden windows, Birgi is on the UNESCO World Heritage Tentative List. On this site you can explore its historic buildings, history and culture.
