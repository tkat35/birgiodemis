Historic Sites / Hamams

Sasalı Hamam

Show on map

On the left side of the road leading to Asartepe, where the remains of the old Birgi castle lie, the ruins of an old hamam from the early Ottoman period stand out. The building has no inscription; inside, its spaces are linked by arches and its corners are decorated with muqarnas. Because the hamam was later used as an oil press, it is also known locally as the “Çukur Yağhane” (Sunken Oil Press). Since it served as an oil press, it contains a large storage jar (küp), jar fragments and a large stone mortar (dibek). The walls are built of rubble stone and broken brick. According to legend, forty girls were caught in a flood while they were in the hamam, so it is also known locally as the Kırk Kızlar (Forty Girls) Hamam. It is said to have been built in the 15th century.
