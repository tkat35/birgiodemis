Historic Sites / Tombs

Aydınoğlu Mehmet Bey Tomb

Show on map

One of the landmark buildings of Birgi, the Mehmet Bey Tomb stands west of the Ulu Cami. Evliya Çelebi describes it as follows: “The benefactor is buried inside the luminous dome in front of the right side door of the mosque. All its walls are made of white raw marble, no other stone. Its dome is covered with lead.” According to its inscription, it was built in 1334 for Aydınoğlu Mehmet Bey. The walls of the square, domed building are faced with reused marble blocks, and its dome is covered with brick. The dome rests on an octagonal drum. The tomb is entered through a slightly pointed arched doorway faced with marble. The gravestones bear no dates, only the names of the deceased. Inside the tomb are the graves of Aydınoğlu Mehmet Bey and his sons Bahadır Bey, İsa Bey and Umur Bey.
