Historic Sites / Mansions

Kerimağa Mansion

Show on map

One of the original examples of traditional Turkish domestic architecture, in which the building techniques of its time can be read, the mansion was probably built in the 18th or 19th century. The ground floor is built of rubble stone with earth mortar; the upper floor projects over the street, and its timber-framed walls are filled with rubble stone laid in earth mortar and covered with earth plaster mixed with straw. On the upper floor, the open hall (sofa) facing the garden is surrounded by rooms. On the ground-floor walls, whose street corner is rounded, wooden brackets (elibelinde) ending in pomegranate and plant motifs support the projecting consoles.
