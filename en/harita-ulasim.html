Getting there

Included by UNESCO on the World Heritage Tentative List and selected in 2022 by the World Tourism Organization as one of the 32 best tourism villages, Birgi is a place to discover, with its stone-walled, red-tiled, wooden-windowed houses rising among the green leaves of centuries-old plane and walnut trees.

Built on both banks of the Sarıyar stream and holding an important place thanks to its historical and cultural heritage, Birgi is 10 km from Ödemiş. Ödemiş, 110 km from the centre of İzmir, is reached by leaving the İzmir–Aydın motorway at the Ödemiş exit. Coming along the Ankara–İzmir road, you can also reach Ödemiş by turning off at Salihli towards Bozdağ and Ödemiş.

Minibuses leave the centre of Ödemiş for Birgi every half hour.

If you fly to İzmir, you can reach Ödemiş by renting a car at Adnan Menderes Airport or by taking the Basmane–Ödemiş regional train, which passes through the airport.

The Basmane–Ödemiş train from İzmir Basmane runs seven times a day in each direction. You can come to the district on one of these trains.

If you prefer to travel by bus, bus companies from the İzmir Intercity Bus Terminal also run services to the district.

You can also take the İZBAN suburban train from one of İzmir's metro stations towards Menderes, get off at Torbalı station and reach the district by the 795 connecting bus.

Where to stay

In Birgi, which is on the UNESCO World Heritage Tentative List and home to many historic buildings, accommodation is mostly in boutique hotels and guesthouses located in historic houses and mansions. Most of these have been restored and decorated in keeping with Birgi's historic fabric. They mostly have traditional Ottoman architecture and take care to preserve the historic atmosphere.

Visitors who want to stay in Birgi usually choose places offering bed and breakfast. These places serve a rich breakfast of local delicacies in the morning, and some also offer dinner. Guests staying in Birgi can relax in the gardens or on the terraces of these places, enjoy the view or spend peaceful hours.

Prices vary according to the location, quality and services of the place and the season, and are generally higher in the summer months and at weekends.

Another option for accommodation is the hotels in the centre of Ödemiş. The Teachers' House (Öğretmenevi) in the district may also be among the options.
